# Marketing Media Prompts

Use these prompts for Northline preview media. The generated assets must use
synthetic data only and must not imply public customer traction.

## ChatGPT Images: Hero Poster

```text
Create a polished SaaS product hero poster for Northline, an AI inbound lead handling product for Greek SMBs.

Scene:
A realistic browser-based dashboard on a warm off-white background. The dashboard shows a website chat lead being captured, qualified, and routed. Use synthetic data only.

Main UI elements:
- Left panel: inbox with 3 inbound leads from Website Chat, WhatsApp, Instagram.
- Center panel: active chat conversation in English and Greek.
- Right panel: qualification fields: Need, Timing, Language, Consent, Booking readiness.
- Bottom strip: "Suggested next action: Offer booking times" and "Handoff packet ready".

Style:
Premium B2B SaaS, trustworthy, calm, practical, not futuristic sci-fi. Warm canvas background, deep ink text, teal/blue accents. 8px radius cards, clean typography, realistic spacing. No logos except small "Northline" wordmark. No stock-photo people. No fake customer logos. No exaggerated metrics.

Text rules:
Use minimal readable UI text. Use only these visible phrases:
"Northline"
"Live lead"
"Ready to book"
"Greek + English"
"Handoff packet"
"Booking link suggested"

Composition:
16:9 landscape, suitable as a video poster on a landing page. Leave safe empty space around edges. High contrast, crisp UI, professional marketing quality.
```

## ChatGPT Images: Three Video Keyframes

```text
Create three consistent 16:9 storyboard keyframes for a Northline product demo video. Keep the same visual style, dashboard layout, colors, and synthetic lead data across all frames.

Brand:
Northline, AI inbound lead handling for Greek SMBs. Warm off-white canvas, deep ink text, teal and blue accents, premium B2B SaaS, practical and trustworthy.

Frame 1: The pain
A busy small business inbox with website chat, WhatsApp, and Instagram inquiries piling up after hours. Show subtle labels like "New lead", "Waiting", "After hours". No panic, but clear operational overload.

Frame 2: The solution in action
Northline replies instantly in a website chat, detects Greek/English language, asks qualifying questions, and fills qualification fields in a dashboard.

Frame 3: The outcome
A qualified lead is marked "Ready to book". A handoff packet shows summary, contact, urgency, language, missing details, and suggested next action. A destination panel shows calendar/webhook/CRM ready.

Constraints:
Synthetic data only. No real brands, no fake client logos, no fake testimonials, no people's faces. Keep UI text short and readable. Make it look like product software, not an abstract AI illustration.
```

## Sora: 35-45 Second Product Demo

```text
Create a 40-second premium B2B SaaS product demo video for "Northline", an AI inbound lead handling product for Greek SMBs.

Format:
16:9, clean landing-page hero video, polished product animation, realistic browser UI, no real people, no fake customer logos. Use synthetic data only. Warm off-white background, deep ink typography, teal and blue accents, calm professional motion. The video should feel trustworthy, practical, and production-ready, not sci-fi.

Narrative:
0-6s: Show the pain. A small business has inbound messages arriving from website chat, WhatsApp, and Instagram after hours. Labels show "New lead", "Waiting", "No owner", "Needs response". The mood is operational pressure, not chaos.

6-16s: Northline activates. A website chat conversation opens. The assistant replies quickly, detects English/Greek, and asks focused follow-up questions. Show fields filling in: Need, Timing, Language, Consent.

16-27s: Qualification happens. The lead becomes "Ready to book". A booking recommendation appears: "Offer booking times". A clean qualification score or status appears, but do not show unrealistic revenue metrics.

27-36s: Handoff and sync. A handoff packet appears with summary, missing details, suggested next action. Destination cards show Calendar, CRM/Webhook, Human handoff as ready states.

36-40s: End card. Northline wordmark, headline: "Turn inbound messages into qualified next steps." CTA text: "Request preview".

Visual constraints:
- No third-party logos.
- No fake testimonials.
- No human faces.
- No claim that the product is fully launched.
- Use "Preview workflow" subtly if a label is needed.
- UI text should be short and readable.
- Keep motion smooth: cards slide, fields fill, statuses update, cursor/tap interactions are subtle.
- Include soft interface sounds only if audio is generated; no voiceover required.
```
